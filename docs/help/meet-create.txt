Usage: meet create --room <name> (--on <date> | --from <ts> --until <ts>) [--note ...]

Register a meeting room. The URL becomes joinable from --from until --until
for guests; moderator-JWT visits bypass the window.

Timestamps are RFC3339 (e.g. 2026-05-22T19:00:00Z) or dates
(e.g. 2026-05-22, interpreted as 00:00:00Z).

Use --on YYYY-MM-DD for an all-day room from 00:00:00Z to 23:59:59Z.

Options:
  --room <name>       Room name to register. Required.
  --on <date>         Meeting date as YYYY-MM-DD; creates an all-day window
                      from 00:00:00Z to 23:59:59Z.
  --from <timestamp>  Valid-from time, as RFC3339 or YYYY-MM-DD.
  --until <timestamp> Valid-until time, as RFC3339 or YYYY-MM-DD.
  --note <text>       Free-form note stored with the registry event.
  --config <paths>    Comma-separated config files. Defaults to auto-discovery
                      from CONFIG_PATH, SECRETS_PATH, and local config files.
