Usage: meet cancel --room <name> [--note ...]

Cancel a registered meeting room. After cancellation the URL is no longer
joinable, even within its original time window.

Options:
  --room <name>       Room name to cancel. Required.
  --note <text>       Free-form note stored with the cancellation event.
  --config <paths>    Comma-separated config files. Defaults to auto-discovery
                      from CONFIG_PATH, SECRETS_PATH, and local config files.
