Usage:
  meet list [--filter current|all|active|upcoming|past|cancelled]
  meet list --room <name> [--count N]

Without --room, lists rooms. The default filter is "current": rooms active now
or in the future (one-off rooms not yet past, and recurring rooms not past their
--ends). Use --filter all to include past and cancelled rooms.

With --room, lists that room's upcoming occurrences (for a recurring room) or
its window (for a one-off), up to --count occurrences (default 6).

Options:
  --filter <status>   Rooms to show: current (default), all, active, upcoming,
                      past, or cancelled.
  --room <name>       List this room's upcoming occurrences instead of all rooms.
  --count <n>         Max occurrences to show with --room (default 6).
  --config <paths>    Comma-separated config files. Defaults to auto-discovery
                      from CONFIG_PATH, SECRETS_PATH, and local config files.
